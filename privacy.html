Privacy Policy

Last updated 15 November 2026

01

The short version

We only collect what we need to run the server, the Discord and the store. We never see or store your card details, and this website uses no advertising or tracking cookies.

02

What we collect

  • In game: your Minecraft username and UUID, your IP address as logged by the server software, chat messages and server logs.
  • In Discord: your username, ID, roles and anything you write in tickets.
  • From the store: the name, email and receipt reference Stripe gives us, and the Minecraft username you give for delivery.
03

Services we use

  • Stripe, for payments.
  • Discord, for the community and support tickets.
  • GitHub Pages, which hosts this website and may log your IP address.
  • Google Fonts, which loads the site's font and receives your IP address.
04

Why we keep it

To run the server and deliver your purchases, to moderate and enforce bans, to sort out payment problems, and to meet legal and accounting duties.

05

How long we keep it

Server logs and moderation records are kept as long as they're useful for moderation. Ban records may be kept indefinitely. Ticket and purchase records are kept as long as needed for the purchase and our accounts.

06

Your rights

You can ask what we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it by opening a Discord ticket. We'll only refuse deletion when we need a record to enforce an active ban or to meet a legal duty related to a payment. Please allow a few days for a reply.

07

Age

This site isn't aimed at children under 13. If a child's data has been given to us, we'll remove it on request. Anyone buying needs permission from whoever owns the payment method.

08

Security

Only staff who need your data can see it. No system is perfectly secure, so please report anything suspicious in a ticket. Staff will never ask for your password, card number or a login code.

09

Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest change. Privacy questions go to a Discord ticket.

Questions about this policy? Open a ticket in our Discord.
