Frequently asked questions

Can't find your answer? Open a ticket in our Discord.

What is Monsieur SMP?

A Minecraft survival server. Everything in the store is cosmetic or a convenience. Nothing we sell gives a fighting advantage or ownership of anything on the server.

How do I get my items?

Pay on the store page, then take a screenshot or photo of your payment. Open a ticket in our Discord and send it with your Minecraft username. Staff deliver your items by hand.

What payments do you accept?

Card payments through Stripe. We never see or store your card details.

Can I get a refund?

No. Store items are digital and non-refundable once they've been delivered. We'll look at a few specific cases, which are listed on the Refund Policy page.

How does Monsieur+ work?

Monsieur+ is £4 a month. It gives you extra auction, home and order slots, and 1,000 shards every month while the rank is active. It renews until you cancel.

How do I cancel Monsieur+?

Open a Discord ticket and ask to cancel. You keep the rank until the end of the month you've already paid for.

What are shards?

Shards are an in-game currency. Shard packs are delivered to your account in-game after payment.

What is the server IP?

monsieur.smp.quest. Java Edition. Join the Discord for news and updates.

Are mods allowed?

No. Hacked clients and cheat mods are banned, including movement, inventory, radar, freecam and ESP mods. See the Rules page for the full list.

My payment went through but I got nothing. What now?

Open a Discord ticket with your Stripe receipt, your Minecraft username and what you expected to receive.

Before you pay

Keep proof of your purchase

Make sure you take a screenshot or picture as proof of your purchase, then open a ticket in our Discord.
