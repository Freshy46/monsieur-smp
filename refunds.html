Refund Policy

Last updated 15 November 2026

PrivacyTermsRefunds
01

The short version

Everything in the store is digital: shard packs, crate keys and the Monsieur+ rank. Once an item has been delivered to your account, it can't be refunded. Buying something is a way of supporting the server, and the item is our thanks.

02

When we will look at a refund

  • You were charged twice for the same item.
  • You paid, but the item was never delivered and we can't deliver it.
  • A technical fault charged you for something you didn't choose.

Open a ticket with your Stripe receipt or screenshot. We'll either deliver what you paid for or refund it.

03

When we won't refund

  • You changed your mind after the item was delivered.
  • You gave the wrong Minecraft username. We'll move the item to the right account instead.
  • You were banned or punished for breaking the rules.
  • You stopped playing, or the server went offline, reset, changed or closed.
  • A perk, item or feature was changed or removed.
04

Monsieur+ subscription

Monsieur+ renews every month until you cancel. You can cancel at any time and keep the rank until the end of the month you've paid for. Past months aren't refunded, and the monthly 1,000 shards stop when the rank ends.

05

How to raise a problem

Open a ticket in our Discord. Include your Stripe receipt or the screenshot you took when you paid, your exact Minecraft username and what you expected to get. Approved refunds go back to your original payment method through Stripe and can take a few working days.

06

Chargebacks

Please talk to us before raising a chargeback or payment dispute. A chargeback opened without contacting us first removes anything you bought and gets you permanently banned from the store.

07

Your legal rights

Nothing here affects consumer rights that can't be excluded by law. Your right to cancel a digital purchase can end once delivery starts with your agreement, which for us is when the item reaches your account. If you think a legal right applies, say so in your ticket.

Questions about this policy? Open a ticket in our Discord.
